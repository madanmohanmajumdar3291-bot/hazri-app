# Hazri Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Hazri is a face-recognition attendance app for businesses. This policy explains what personal data Hazri handles, why, where it is stored, and the rights people have over it. It is written to meet India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

## Who is responsible

- **The employer** (the company that signs up and adds its staff) decides whose data is collected and why. Under the DPDP Act the employer is the Data Fiduciary for its employees' data.
- **Hazri** runs the app and stores the data on the employer's behalf, and only uses it to provide the service.

Contact for privacy questions and grievances: **[CONTACT EMAIL]**. We reply within 7 days.

## What we collect

| Data | Whose | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Name, phone, email, employee code, department, shift | Employees, managers, owners | Accounts and attendance records |
| Face photo and face template (numbers that describe the face) | Anyone whose face is registered | Recognise the person at check-in and confirm identity for protected actions |
| Check-in and check-out times | Employees | Attendance, late marks, half days, reports |
| Location at check-in | Employees | Check that attendance is marked at a work site, when the employer turns on site checking |
| Live location during work hours | Field or remote employees who have agreed | Field visit tracking and movement timeline, only after separate location consent in the app |
| Salary, overtime rate, ID proof type and number | Employees | Payroll and records, visible only to the owner and admins |
| Visit photos | Field employees | Proof of field visits |

We do not collect contacts, messages, or files from the phone, and we do not sell or share personal data for advertising.

## Consent

Before a face is registered, the app asks for consent. When an admin registers someone else's face, the admin must confirm that the person was told and agreed. The time of consent is recorded. Live location tracking has its own separate consent.

Consent can be withdrawn at any time by asking the employer to remove the face registration or the employee. Face data is then deleted as described below.

## Where data is stored

- **Supabase** (database, login and file storage). Face photos are stored in private storage. Only signed-in members of the same company can view them, through links that expire after one hour.
- **Amazon Web Services Rekognition** stores a face template used for face matching, separated per company.
- **Google Drive**: only if the employer turns on backup. The backup goes to the employer's own Google Drive account.

Data may be stored on servers outside India, as the DPDP Act permits.

## How long we keep it

- Face photos and face templates are deleted when the face is re-registered (the old one is replaced), when the employee is removed, or when the company account is closed.
- Attendance records are kept while the company account is active, so the employer can meet its own payroll and labour-law record keeping. They are deleted within 90 days after the company account is closed.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Each company's data is separated with database access rules, so one company cannot read another's data. Passwords are handled by the login provider and are never stored by Hazri in readable form.

## Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can ask to:

- see the personal data held about you,
- correct or update it,
- have it erased,
- name someone to act for you,
- raise a grievance.

Employees should usually ask their employer first. You can also write to **[CONTACT EMAIL]**. If you are not satisfied with the answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

## Children

Hazri is for workplaces and is not meant for anyone under 18.
